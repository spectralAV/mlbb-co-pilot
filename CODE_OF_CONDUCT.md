# Code Of Conduct

MLBB Co-Pilot should be a practical and respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear, patient, and constructive.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Credit sources, datasets, models, and third-party projects accurately.
- Avoid sharing private logs, tokens, gameplay footage, or unlicensed assets.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Requests to bypass game protections, abuse services, or compromise accounts.
- Spam, repeated off-topic comments, or bad-faith issue reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and issues that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in blocked participation.
